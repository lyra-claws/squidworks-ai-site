01 / The approach

A little clarity.
A useful next step.

You don’t need to figure out everything at once. Start with a real question from your business, then make room to learn.

  1. 01

    Understand the basics.

    Plain-language explanations of what AI does, where it can help, and where to be careful.

  2. 02

    Connect it to your work.

    Start with something familiar: an idea to explore, a draft to improve, or a task you’d like to understand better.

  3. 03

    Keep your judgment in the loop.

    Try a small experiment. Check the result. Decide what’s useful and what deserves a second look.
